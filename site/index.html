A few things to know.

Does it move or copy my files?

oShelf keeps references to files and folders. It never moves or deletes the originals. Outgoing drags use copy semantics; the receiving application decides what to do with the offered data.

Will my shelf survive a restart?

No. Shelf contents disappear when the plugin reloads or the shell session ends. Your placement, size, and timing preferences are saved separately.

Can I drag anything into any app?

Files, folders, bundles, links, text, and portable image data are supported. Destinations choose which formats they accept. Image-only data is not converted into a file, and browser, GTK, and sandboxed app compatibility is not exhaustive.

Is this part of Omarchy?

oShelf is an independent community plugin for Omarchy Quattro. It is free software under the MIT license.
